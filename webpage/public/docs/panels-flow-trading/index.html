Flow & Trading Panels

These panels explain who is trading, where fills are clustering, whether liquidity is thin or real, and which external trading venues may be pressuring Polymarket probabilities. They are built around OrderFilled rows, wallet flow, cross-market signals, and crypto or finance pressure boards.

OrderFilled Flow

Panel What it shows How to use it
Orderfilled Flow
global-orderfilled		 Cross-market latest on-chain OrderFilled activity with market, side, price, size, and recency context. Use it as the live tape for the whole platform, then pivot into the specific market that is moving.
Whale Tracker
whale-tracker		 Largest recent on-chain trades, emphasizing high-notional or high-impact wallet behavior. Use it to detect concentrated flow that may not be obvious from price history alone.
Flow Watch
suspicious-flow		 Oracle-adjacent and large live trade flow that deserves extra review. Use it when a market is near close, near resolution, or moving on unusual fill activity.
PolyBeats
polybeats-feed		 Smart-money flow, wallet history, and explainable market briefs. Use it to connect wallet activity with a readable market thesis instead of staring at raw addresses.

Signal Panels

Panel What it shows How to use it
Alpha Signal
alpha-signal		 Cross-source heuristic signal stack combining price action, flow, external content, and market context. Use it as a triage layer: it does not replace research, but it tells you which markets deserve attention first.
AI Special Markets
sample-chain-trades		 Market-wide AI radar for unusual, high-attention, and fast-moving markets. Use it to find special situations that are not captured by a single category filter.

Crypto and Finance Flow

Panel What it shows How to use it
Crypto Watch
crypto-watch		 Crypto spot trends and sparklines across major assets. Use it when crypto prices are likely to explain probability movement in crypto-linked Polymarket markets.
Crypto Funding Watch
crypto-funding-watch		 Cross-venue perpetual funding heatmap with long/short crowding bias. Use it to identify crowded positioning before it appears in prediction-market odds.
Crypto Perps
crypto-perp-funding		 Crypto perpetual funding pressure list. Use it as a compact ranking of markets where derivatives positioning is most stretched.
TradFi Perps
tradfi-perp-radar		 Tokenized stock, index, and commodity perpetual radar. Use it to connect synthetic-market pressure to Polymarket questions about equities, indices, and commodities.
Crypto ETF
crypto-etf-flow		 BTC and ETH ETF flow proxy board. Use it for institutional flow context around Bitcoin, Ethereum, and ETF-related event markets.
Fear & Greed
crypto-fear-greed		 Crypto sentiment gauge with price drivers. Use it to distinguish broad sentiment pressure from market-specific news.
Stablecoins
stablecoin-monitor		 Stablecoin peg and supply monitor. Use it when market movement depends on crypto liquidity, depeg risk, or stablecoin supply changes.
DeFi Token Watch
defi-token-watch		 Compact DeFi token tape with price, 24h move, and weekly trend. Use it to monitor protocol tokens that may affect DeFi, governance, or exploit-related markets.
DeFi Yields
defi-yield-monitor		 Protocol APY and TVL watch board. Use it to track yield stress, capital rotation, and protocol-level risk appetite.
