Intelligence & News Panels

These panels explain why a market may be moving and whether its resolution path is clean. They combine oracle state, related news, AI summaries, policy feeds, finance news, technology signals, and domain-specific event feeds.

Oracle and AI Analysis

Panel What it shows How to use it
Oracle Feed
oracle-feed		 Recent oracle events across markets, including resolution-adjacent activity. Use it to verify whether market status is still trading-driven or already in an oracle/resolution phase.
AI Market Brief
price-implications		 Market-wide AI brief, focal points, and convergence signals. Use it to compress multiple market signals into a short analyst-readable thesis.
AI Trend Radar
oracle-timeline		 Market-wide AI synthesis of trend clusters, catalysts, and watch items. Use it to spot clusters of related markets moving around the same catalyst or narrative.
Related News
related-news		 News linked to the focused market. Use it to connect a probability move to a source, headline, or external event without leaving the workspace.

Technology and Finance Intelligence

Panel What it shows How to use it
AI Model Race
ai-model-race		 AI lab release, benchmark, pricing, valuation, outage, and regulation signal feed. Use it for AI-company and model-release markets where product news moves probability quickly.
Big Tech Cap
big-tech-market-cap		 Mega-cap technology quote and market-cap rank board. Use it to anchor tech-market narratives in live equity context rather than only headline flow.
App Pulse
consumer-app-pulse		 App Store, Google Play, TikTok, consumer ranking, and regulation signals. Use it for consumer internet markets where adoption, ranking, bans, or platform changes matter.
IPO News
ipo-news-watch		 IPO, listing, and filing catalyst feed. Use it for listing, company, valuation, and deal-timing questions.
Broker Research
broker-research-watch		 Analyst rating and target-price change radar. Use it to detect TradFi analyst catalysts that may affect equity-linked or sector-linked markets.

Policy, Risk, and Event Feeds

Panel What it shows How to use it
Chain Policy
blockchain-policy-news		 Blockchain policy, regulation, and enforcement feed. Use it for crypto regulation, enforcement, ETF, and protocol governance markets.
DeFi Security
defi-security-watch		 DeFi exploit, vulnerability, and protocol risk feed. Use it to identify protocol-specific security shocks before they appear in broader crypto prices.
Trade Policy
trade-policy-radar		 High-signal trade policy watchlist mapped to tariffs, flows, barriers, revenue, and strategic controls. Use it when market movement depends on duties, export controls, sanctions, or supply-chain rerouting.
Geopolitical & Sanctions Shock
geo-sanctions-shock		 Geopolitical shocks, sanctions changes, and linked macro-risk markets. Use it to connect global event risk to commodity, macro, election, and security markets.
Jin10 Flash
jin10-flash		 Jin10 macro flash feed in market-card form. Use it for fast macro headlines that can move rates, inflation, FX, and commodity questions.
BWE News
f1-trackside		 BWENews runtime RSS flashes with cached auto-refresh. Use it as a compact live news feed for fast-moving event surfaces that need headline triage.
