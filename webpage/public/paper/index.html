Turning Polymarket data into a live intelligence workspace.

polyData Monitor combines market state, on-chain flow, oracle activity, order-book depth, external context, and AI briefs into one operational surface for prediction-market analysis.

Research Brief

Problem Signals are fragmented

Traders and researchers switch between markets, chain events, news, spreadsheets, and internal notes to explain a move.

Method Unify the event trail

The system links prices, liquidity, OrderFilled flow, oracle state, market groups, and related external content.

Output Readable market context

Panels compress raw data into live rows, watch tags, charts, and briefs that can be scanned quickly.
